FAQ

Borehole and survey questions we hear often

Why do I need a geophysical survey before drilling a borehole?

A survey such as Vertical Electrical Sounding (VES) maps the layers beneath your site, so drilling targets the most promising water-bearing zone and the risk of a dry or low-yield borehole is reduced.

Which areas do you cover?

We work from our Abeokuta (Ogun) and Osogbo (Osun) branches, covering Abeokuta, Ijebu-Ode, Ifo, Ilaro, Osogbo, Ede, Ilesha and Iragbiji. Other South-West locations can be discussed on request.

How much does borehole drilling cost?

Cost depends on depth, local geology, casing and the type of borehole you need. Send us your location and requirements and we will give you a clear quotation.

What is a geotechnical study?

It examines soil and subsurface conditions at a site so engineers can recommend safe foundations before construction begins.
