What we do

Complete groundwater and ground-engineering services

From the first survey to a flowing borehole, one team handles the science and the drilling.

🛰️

Geophysical Investigation

Electrical resistivity surveys that locate productive aquifers before a single metre is drilled.

  • Vertical Electrical Sounding (VES)
  • Electrical resistivity profiling
  • Depth and yield interpretation
  • Site report with drilling advice
💧

Borehole Drilling

Professional drilling for homes, estates, farms, schools, industries and communities.

  • Residential and commercial boreholes
  • Casing, screening and gravel packing
  • Borehole development and testing
  • Handover with technical details
🏗️

Geotechnical Studies

Understand your soil and subsurface conditions before you build.

  • Subsurface and soil investigation
  • Foundation recommendations
  • Site suitability assessment
  • Clear engineering reports
